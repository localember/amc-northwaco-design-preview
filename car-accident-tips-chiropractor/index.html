Chiropractor has tips for auto accidents in North Waco

What to do in the hours and days after a crash, the symptoms that show up late, and when to get checked.

See New Patient SpecialsCall (254) 799-8343
The short answer

After any car accident, get emergency care first if you need it, then watch for neck pain, headaches, stiffness, or tingling over the next few days. These symptoms often show up late. A prompt check helps you understand what happened and gives you a record if you need one later.

A crash on I-35, on Loop 340 near the H-E-B and Walmart, or in a parking lot in Bellmead can leave you shaken even when everyone walks away. Adrenaline hides pain in the first hours. Here is what to do next.

Right after the accident

  1. Check for injuries and call 911 if neededSevere pain, trouble breathing, confusion, numbness, or weakness means call 911. Do not move someone unless they are in danger.
  2. Get to a safe spotIf the cars can move and no one is hurt, get out of traffic and turn on the hazard lights.
  3. Exchange information and take photosNames, phone numbers, insurance, and plates. Photos of the cars, the road, and any visible injuries.
  4. Get checked, even if you feel fine tonightNeck and back strain often announces itself the next morning. Our North Waco office sees people after the emergency visit, not instead of it.

Symptoms that show up late

  • Neck stiffness or a headache that starts at the base of the skull
  • Pain between the shoulders or in the low back
  • Tingling, numbness, or weakness in an arm or leg
  • Dizziness, trouble concentrating, or sleep that will not settle

A sudden worst headache of your life, bowel or bladder changes, or weakness that is getting worse needs emergency care, not a chiropractic visit.

How this office fits

After the emergency needs are handled, an exam can look for the mechanical part of the injury. Care here is drug-free and starts with the exam. We do not promise a timeline. The car accident page covers what a visit is like.
